### 🔍 How It Started
I started poking at web apps a few years ago out of curiosity more than anything else — trying to break login forms, messing with request parameters, and seeing what happened when I sent something the developer clearly didn't expect. Somewhere along the way, that curiosity turned into a habit, and the habit turned into this blog.

### 🪲 Inside the Write-ups
Most of what you'll find here are write-ups from bugs I've reported through bug bounty programs, cleaned up and anonymized so they can't be tied back to a specific company or disclosure. I'm not naming targets, dates, or anything that could get someone in trouble — the point isn't to call anyone out, it's to show the actual thought process behind finding a vulnerability, because that's the part most reports and CTF walkthroughs skip over.

### 🧠 Showing the "Boring" Parts
I remember how hard it was starting out — reading a hundred blog posts that all say *"I found an IDOR"* without ever explaining how they noticed the pattern in the first place. 

Because of that, each write-up here tries to walk through the boring parts too:
* **What made me look twice** at a specific request.
* **What didn't work** before something finally did.
* **What I'd do differently** next time.

### 🚫 No Hype, Just Reality
This isn't a place for hype or clickbait CVE announcements. If a bug is interesting because of how simple it turned out to be, or because of how many dead ends I hit before finding it, that's the version I'll write. I'd rather be useful to someone learning than impressive to someone scrolling.

### 🤝 Let's Connect
If you're just getting into bug bounty or web security in general, I hope something here saves you a few hours of confusion. 

> **Got feedback?** If you spot a mistake in one of my write-ups or think I explained something poorly, please tell me — I'd genuinely rather fix it than leave it wrong.
